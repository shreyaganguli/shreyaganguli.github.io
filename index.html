NASA Johnson Space Center Pre-college Research
3D-Printed Forearm Model for Arm Offloading Research

Developed a patient-realistic forearm model at NASA JSC to support arm offloading device testing. Evaluated iPhone and Android 3D scanning apps, processed scan data in Creo Parametric, and redesigned the model to include an internal 1"×1" aluminum channel, a precision IMU mounting surface with caliper-measured bolt holes, and through-hole fasteners. Printed in ABS on a Raise3D Pro2 with custom settings to prevent warping — significantly improving IMU stability and sensor accuracy for offloading algorithm development.

3D Scanning Creo Parametric ABS Printing IMU Sensors Tolerance Design
UConn Engineering Summer Program Pre-college
Automated Knee Exoskeleton Brace

Designed and built a functional knee exoskeleton brace covering the full engineering design cycle: hand-sketched dimensioned drawings, cardboard prototypes, fabrication, electronics integration, and software control. The final brace integrates an Arduino microcontroller, a load cell for force sensing, and an ultrasonic distance sensor, programmed in C++ and Python to automate assistive actuation based on real-time sensor feedback.

Turtle-Themed Combat Robot

Designed and built a turtle-themed combat robot for a team competition. Contributed SolidWorks CAD of a custom wheel (Ø55mm, angled tread, press-fit axle hub) and elliptical shell/armor. Biomedical · Assistive Tech 2025
Arthritis Door-Opening Aid

Portable ergonomic device for rheumatoid and osteoarthritis patients who struggle to grip keys or doorknobs. Backed by 7 peer-reviewed sources. One end holds a key via friction fit; the other uses interlocking C-shaped TPU arms that auto-close around a doorknob when pressed, converting a push into a firm pain-free grip. PLA chassis for rigidity, TPU arms for compliance. Developed through HMW ideation, literature research, sketching, and multi-material 3D prototyping.
