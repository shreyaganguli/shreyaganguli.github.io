NASA Johnson Space Center 2024
NylonX Rope Guide — ISS ARED Wear Solution

Identified an on-orbit hardware damage risk on the ISS ARED resistive exercise machine: the load-bearing rope had no dedicated wear interface, causing direct abrasion against structural components under cyclic load during every astronaut workout. Left unaddressed, this would degrade the rope on an active flight mission with no easy replacement path.

Designed a rope guide from scratch to concentrate all rope contact onto a single interchangeable sacrificial component. Evaluated NylonX, PLA, and ABS for wear resistance and mass before selecting NylonX. Modeled the geometry in Creo Parametric and iterated through multiple prototype cycles on a Raise3D Pro2, testing fit and function at each step. Presented the final design at a formal design review with senior flight systems engineers at JSC. When the guide wears out, it swaps out in minutes without affecting the rest of the system.

Creo Parametric NylonX / FDM Printing Material Selection Raise3D Pro2 Iterative Prototyping Flight Hardware

Structural Analysis · Independent Study 2026
Tube Frame Joint FEA — Triangulation Study

Performed a static structural FEA study on a welded AISI 4130 chromoly steel tube frame T-joint under 500 N bending load, modeling the joint geometry used in FSAE spaceframe construction. Ran two design studies — baseline T-joint and a triangulated configuration — to quantify the structural effect of diagonal bracing. Validated FEA results against analytical bending theory (σ = Mc/I), achieving 0.2% agreement. Adding a single diagonal brace reduced peak Von Mises stress by 44% (258 MPa → 144 MPa) and increased the factor of safety from 1.78 to 3.19, demonstrating the core structural principle behind race car chassis triangulation.

Biomedical · Assistive Tech 2025
Arthritis Door-Opening Aid

Portable ergonomic device for rheumatoid and osteoarthritis patients who struggle to grip keys or doorknobs. Backed by 7 peer-reviewed sources. One end holds a key via friction fit; the other uses interlocking C-shaped TPU arms that auto-close around a doorknob when pressed, converting a push into a firm pain-free grip. PLA chassis for rigidity, TPU arms for compliance. Developed through HMW ideation, literature research, sketching, and multi-material 3D prototyping.
